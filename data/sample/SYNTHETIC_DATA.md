# Synthetic inventory pack

Entirely invented, deterministic hackathon data; no real customer records.
Cutoff: 2026-10-31. Currency: illustrative USD throughout.
24 canonical SKUs, 48 separate warehouse holdings, five intentional bad records, 50 labelled matches.
Demand and safety stock are canonical/global values repeated per holding; they are not summed.
Open PO quantities are per holding. Do not load mirrored stock copies as separate physical holdings.
The baseline is a synthetic reference using a 3-month horizon, 10 safety units and lifecycle flags.
It validates arithmetic and scope handling, not a real company's legacy policy.
Transfer recovery is an illustrative internal utilization value, not external cash proceeds.
No representative production precision, savings, or speed claim can be made from this pack.
