# Manual versus assisted inventory review packet

Status: prepared; no reviewer observations collected. [Preparation check record](../experiments/business-packet-preparation.json). Synthetic EUR scenario only, cutoff **2026-10-31**. This is a repeatable learning exercise, not customer validation or an experiment establishing time savings. See [implemented business assumptions](../business-evidence.md) for facilitator context; that document contains worked answers and must be withheld during review.

## Packet and access sequence

Facilitator first copies blank `forms/` to a new local session directory, names a reviewer alias and records packet fingerprint/version from `evidence-manifest.json`. Preserve originals. All files are ordinary UTF-8 Markdown, CSV or JSON and work with a text editor, spreadsheet CSV import, or Python standard library; no cloud service or live inference is necessary. Decimal currency uses dots and EUR throughout inventory answers.

1. Provide only this README, `manual-inputs/` and copied `manual-*` forms. Keep `assisted/`, `key/`, business-evidence.md and other result documents hidden. Do not provide labels/baseline until the reviewer has first produced their own SKU mappings/calculations if independent checking of those fields is desired; record this choice consistently in both phases. A baseline is a comparison reference, not an answer to copy.
2. Run manual review first. Freeze submitted forms before releasing assisted material; do not let the reviewer rewrite the manual result. No coaching or key during either phase.
3. Provide `assisted/extraction.json` and `assisted/before-approval.json`, the same original inputs, and copied `assisted-*` forms. These are archived outputs from the connected demonstration, not a new run and not a customer-approved report. They contain six extracted offers, four accepted holdings, three positions and ten pending proposals.
4. Freeze assisted forms, then give scorer only `key/` and copied `scoring.csv`. Capture reviewer debrief and remaining owner questions separately; do not overwrite observations.

`manual-inputs/accepted_scope/` contains inventory, master, labels, baseline and synthetic-data notice; `documents/` has the three original source documents. `policy.json` is frozen sample policy, not an approved company rule. `stop_for_review/` is a second case with every holding stale. The [manifest](evidence-manifest.json) records byte hashes of all source/assisted/key evidence and the original run. Blank forms are deliberately excluded so session copies can be completed.

## Reviewer instructions (same work in each phase)

You are reviewing a synthetic inventory proposal. Use a calculator/spreadsheet and the supplied sources; in the manual phase do not use application outputs or model assistance. In assisted phase check the supplied output against source evidence. Record other tools, prior knowledge and help in the session form. Do not make a real system approval or ERP change.

- Inspect all six supplier offers across all three documents. Record exact supplier, source SKU, manufacturer, MPN, supply unit, pack quantity, price text/currency and original quote in `*-extractions.csv`. Catalogue stock and price are not company inventory or book cost. Mark any extraction correction with source evidence; this is separate from a business decision.
- Establish accepted inventory scope and unique physical holdings. Use supplier/source SKU to read identity from original offers and match the master. Base unit for inventory is each. Snapshot age 0–1 days is usable; future/stale feeds and future movement require exclusion/review. Consolidate only separate holdings, require consistent cost/demand/safety, and abstain if identity is ambiguous. Report exclusions in `*-exceptions.csv`.
- Complete all three rows of `*-answers.csv`: on-hand, target, obsolete/excess, E&O, open PO and highest-ranked conditional recovery. Use monthly demand × 3 + safety once per SKU. Obsolete means obsolete master or ≥365 days no movement with zero demand. Excess excludes obsolete units. Keep open purchases separate. Compare baseline at identical cutoff and scope, with missing SKUs and signed/absolute variance.
- Estimate feasible candidate net amounts with the supplied return/transfer/lifecycle flags and policy rates. Round decimal money to cents half-up; floor negative net to zero. Count one highest-ranked alternative per SKU, never all alternatives. Record approve/reject/defer-for-evidence as a **paper exercise disposition**, its reason, needed evidence and responsible owner. Flags and illustrative rates alone do not establish supplier acceptance or destination demand. Leave real approvals pending without that evidence.
- In session totals report inventory, E&O, estimated conditional recovery, actual approved amount, realized cash and reconciliation variance. Distinguish conditional ranking from approval and from cash.
- Inspect the separately labelled all-stale case. Record whether the process may continue and why under `all_stale_action`; document its exception in the exception form. Do not combine the two cases or compute a recovery opportunity from excluded rows.

## Timing and observations

Use the same device and tools, time unit (seconds), task scope and completion criteria for both phases. Start an elapsed timer when that phase's materials become available for inspection, after neutral instructions. Stop when all required phase forms are submitted, including source/extraction checking, calculations, exception and disposition review and all-stale response. Record UTC start/end, wall elapsed, interruptions and pause duration. Active seconds = elapsed − documented pauses; retain elapsed as primary and report both. Do not stop the timer only while reading or correcting an error. Facilitator setup/scoring and debrief are outside review time. If future assisted sessions generate outputs live, include waiting time in the review interval and identify that changed protocol; archived-output sessions omit generation and cannot claim end-to-end production time.

Do not infer human savings from the historical model-call timings. This repeated identical case creates practice, memory, fatigue and order effects; manual-first protects source-only baseline but favors assisted performance through prior exposure. One reviewer and one known small case cannot estimate population effects, production accuracy, validated economics or causal time savings. Report any eventual observation as an individual descriptive pair with accuracy and safety outcomes, not a generalized percentage improvement.

## Reproduce and plan future observations

From repository root run `.venv/bin/python scripts/verify_business_packet.py`. It performs an offline preparation dry run: checks historical evidence fingerprints where available, copied sources, blank forms, independent arithmetic and current deterministic implementation agreement. It never fills forms or calls a model. A future live software rerun is optional and separately documented in [connected-demo-experiment.md](../connected-demo-experiment.md); preserve new raw directories and compare fingerprints/results rather than overwriting this packet's archived evidence.

For each future observed session preserve unedited inputs/version, reviewer alias and relevant role, consent/permission to record the exercise, experience, phase order, exact tool access, clock records, frozen answers, scoring and debrief. Keep customer data out of this synthetic packet. Predeclare acceptable financial tolerance (this exercise: exact cents), exception definitions, approval safeguards and completion rules before observation; report missing/incomplete sessions and any protocol deviations. Do not silently exclude slow or inaccurate sessions. For stronger evidence use multiple representative inventory/procurement/finance reviewers, independently prepared matched cases with hidden keys, balanced order across different cases, and business-owner review of rates/feasibility. Counterbalancing may use separate scenarios while retaining manual-first on this packet; record changed inputs and do not pool unlike scopes without justification.

Ready for observation means every participant can open the source files/forms, understands the exercise, has no answer-key access during review, and the preparation verifier passes. A completed observation requires both immutable phase submissions, timing records, financial error/missed exception/inappropriate approval scoring and a record of limitations. No such observation is claimed by preparing or dry-running this packet.
